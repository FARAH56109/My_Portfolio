Project 01 — Health Condition Predictor
Artificial Intelligence • Healthcare

Health Condition Predictor

DATA PROCESSING · 01

Prepared health-related inputs using feature encoding, scaling, and BMI calculation.

PREDICTION · 02

Integrated Random Forest, Gaussian Naive Bayes, and an MLP neural network.

RESULTS · 03

Connected model predictions to a desktop interface for presenting risk assessments.
